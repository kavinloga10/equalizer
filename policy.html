Terms & Privacy

Last updated August 2026

Equalizer is a study tool built to help students prepare for the TJHSST and Academies of Loudoun admissions process. This page explains, in plain language, what happens when you use it.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your name, email address, grade, and — if you provide them — your target program and test date. As you use the app, we also store your study activity: questions answered, streaks, points, badges, and mock test results. This is used only to run the app (showing your progress, personalizing what you study next) and isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

Where your data lives

Account and progress data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database provider. Access is restricted so that only you can see your own progress — other students can only see your name and point total on the leaderboard.

AI features

The AI Tutor and essay (SPS) grading are powered by Google's Gemini API. Responses and grades are generated automatically and can be wrong or incomplete — they're a study aid, not an authoritative judgment of your work, and definitely not a guarantee of admission to any program. Always double-check important answers with a teacher or trusted source.

No guarantee of results

Equalizer is a practice and study tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TJHSST, the Academies of Loudoun, LCPS, or any other school or admissions program. Using Equalizer — including any AI-graded practice questions, mock tests, or essay/writing feedback — does not guarantee that you will pass an admissions test, be admitted to any program, or achieve any particular score. The Equalizer team is not responsible for, and is not liable for, a student's test outcomes, admissions decisions, or any other result of using this app. Equalizer is meant to supplement, not replace, your own preparation, judgment, and guidance from teachers, parents, or other trusted resources.

If you're under 13

Equalizer is meant to be used with a parent or guardian's awareness, especially for students under 13. If you're a parent and want to review or remove your child's data, contact us using the email below.

Your account, your data

You can stop using Equalizer at any time. To have your account and all associated data permanently deleted, email kavinloga10@gmail.com from the account's email address and we'll remove it.
